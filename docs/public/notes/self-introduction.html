英语测评 · 5分钟英文自我介绍

中英文对照版 · 适用于英语测评 / 面试场景

第一部分　Self-introduction

自我介绍

English

Good morning, everyone.

My name is Li Qianzhen. I graduated from Wuchang University of Technology, majoring in Computer Science and Technology.

I have over two years of experience in big data development. I have been working on a big data project for China Telecom in Xinjiang.

My main responsibilities include data warehouse development, ETL development, data processing, and database optimization.

In my daily work, I use tools and technologies such as SQL, Python, Spark, Hive, and Doris. I also have experience in data warehouse modeling and data quality management.

I enjoy solving technical problems and learning new technologies. I believe that continuous learning is very important in the IT industry.

中文翻译

大家早上好。

我叫李前振，毕业于武昌理工学院，专业是计算机科学与技术。

我有两年多的大数据开发经验，目前参与中国电信新疆公司的一个大数据项目。

我的主要工作职责包括数据仓库开发、ETL开发、数据处理和数据库优化。

在日常工作中，我使用 SQL、Python、Spark、Hive 和 Doris 等工具和技术。我也有数据仓库建模和数据质量管理方面的经验。

我喜欢解决技术问题和学习新技术。我认为，持续学习对于 IT 行业来说非常重要。

第二部分　My work experience

工作经历

English

In my current job, I mainly work on a data warehouse project for China Telecom.

The project supports several business departments, including marketing and customer service.

My main task is to process business data and build data warehouse models. I use Spark and SQL to clean, transform, and process data.

I also use DolphinScheduler to manage and schedule data tasks.

In addition, I help maintain Doris clusters and optimize SQL queries.

For example, I once worked on a slow query involving more than one billion rows of data.

I analyzed the query and improved the table partitioning and data distribution strategies.

As a result, the query performance improved significantly, and the amount of scanned data was greatly reduced.

This experience helped me understand the importance of data modeling and SQL optimization.

中文翻译

在目前的工作中，我主要参与中国电信的一个数据仓库项目。

该项目为多个业务部门提供支持，包括营销部门和客服部门。

我的主要任务是处理业务数据并构建数据仓库模型。我使用 Spark 和 SQL 对数据进行清洗、转换和处理。

我还使用 DolphinScheduler 管理和调度数据任务。

此外，我也参与 Doris 集群的维护和 SQL 查询优化。

例如，我曾经处理过一个涉及十亿多行数据的慢查询问题。

我分析了查询语句，并改进了表的分区和数据分布策略。

最终，查询性能得到了显著提升，数据扫描量也大幅减少。

这段经历让我认识到数据建模和 SQL 优化的重要性。

第三部分　My strengths

我的优势

English

I think my main strengths are my sense of responsibility, my problem-solving skills, and my willingness to learn.

First, I take my work seriously. When a problem occurs, I try to find the root cause instead of simply fixing the symptoms.

Second, I enjoy learning new technologies. When I face a new technical challenge, I search for information, read documentation, and practice by myself.

Third, I can work well with other team members. I believe communication and teamwork are important for completing a project successfully.

Of course, I still need to improve my English communication skills and my understanding of some advanced technologies.

I am working hard to improve myself step by step.

中文翻译

我认为自己的主要优势是责任心强、具备解决问题的能力，并且愿意学习。

首先，我认真对待自己的工作。当问题发生时，我会努力找到根本原因，而不是仅仅解决表面问题。

其次，我喜欢学习新技术。当遇到新的技术挑战时，我会查找资料、阅读技术文档，并自己动手实践。

第三，我能够与团队成员良好合作。我认为，沟通和团队合作对于成功完成一个项目非常重要。

当然，我仍然需要提高自己的英语沟通能力，并加深对一些先进技术的理解。

我正在努力一步一步地提升自己。

第四部分　My hobbies

我的兴趣爱好

English

In my free time, I enjoy playing basketball and learning new technologies.

Playing basketball helps me stay healthy and reduce stress after work.

I usually play basketball with my friends when we have time.

I also enjoy learning about new technologies, especially big data and data engineering.

Sometimes, I practice SQL or write small Python programs to improve my technical skills.

These hobbies help me maintain a balance between work and personal life.

中文翻译

在空闲时间，我喜欢打篮球和学习新技术。

打篮球可以帮助我保持健康，并缓解工作后的压力。

有时间的时候，我通常会和朋友一起打篮球。

我也喜欢了解新技术，尤其是大数据和数据工程方面的技术。

有时，我会练习 SQL 或编写一些简单的 Python 程序，以提高自己的技术能力。

这些爱好帮助我保持工作与个人生活之间的平衡。

第五部分　My career plan

我的职业规划

English

In the future, I want to become a more professional data warehouse developer.

In the short term, I want to improve my skills in SQL, Spark, data warehouse modeling, and data optimization.

I also want to learn more about real-time data processing and big data architecture.

In the long term, I hope to become an experienced data engineer who can independently design and develop data warehouse solutions.

I believe that practical experience, continuous learning, and teamwork will help me achieve this goal.

中文翻译

未来，我希望成为一名更加专业的数据仓库开发工程师。

短期内，我希望提高自己在 SQL、Spark、数据仓库建模和数据优化方面的技能。

我也希望进一步学习实时数据处理和大数据架构方面的知识。

从长远来看，我希望成为一名经验丰富的数据工程师，能够独立设计和开发数据仓库解决方案。

我相信，实践经验、持续学习和团队合作将帮助我实现这个目标。

总结

English

That's all about me.

I am glad to have this opportunity to introduce myself.

Thank you for your time and attention!
